Research Experience

Sep. 2025 – Present

Research Intern, GAI Lab

Incheon National University · Advisor: Prof. Jibum Kim · Incheon, Korea

  • Self-directed simulation project in Python (manuscript in preparation): with weights held fixed to isolate what dynamics alone provide, can synchronization with a chaotic world, a state of its own, and adaptation emerge in a spiking network?
    • Measured the world-driven and own parts of each neuron’s activity with copies of the network driven by the same world from different starting states.
    • Found that they can: the own part keeps 88% of its spread across a 105-fold rise in input gain, and evolved networks, once frozen, re-synchronize in a median of 11–19 steps after a world switch.
    • Reported the limit: the tested world is simple enough that a linear filter reaches 90% of an oracle; a world that requires the own part is the next step.
  • Lab seminar: reviewed a brain-to-text decoding paper, arguing that teacher forcing leaks target information into its reported scores.
